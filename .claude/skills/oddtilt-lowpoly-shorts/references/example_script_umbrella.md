## Full Voiceover Script (Zero Dead Air)

> You're walking to your date, but it starts pouring rain and ruins your hair. So you hold a flat wooden plank over your head. Clever! Except your arms give out in ten seconds and it snaps your spine. So you replace the plank with a thin sheet of fabric stretched over sticks, and mount it on one central pole. Genius! But the first gust of wind flips it inside out. So you curve the sticks downward and add hinged ribs underneath to lock it open. Clever! Except carrying a giant six-foot pole into a restaurant gets you kicked out. So you make the pole telescope inward and fold the ribs flat against the shaft. Now you can arrive dry—and still get stood up.
