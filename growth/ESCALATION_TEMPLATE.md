# The escalation script template (the user's spec: use it for every new script)

1. **Seed and baseline (0:00–0:05):** If you [simple, low-tech action] to [basic goal], you have a [basic version name].
2. **Loop 1, the obvious flaw (0:05–0:13):** But [enemy / environment / user] [exploits the obvious weakness], so you [first structural upgrade]. This is your [upgraded stage name].
3. **Loop 2, the counter-measure (0:13–0:22):** But [new threat or smarter tactic], so you [modify the build or change material].
4. **Loop 3, the hidden or technical threat (0:22–0:32):** But now they [use a specialized tool or detection method], so you [add an engineering or stealth fix]. This is your [next milestone name].
5. **Loop 4, survival or scale pressure (0:32–0:42):** But [internal need or mass threat] [creates a fatal vulnerability], so you [build a clever workaround].
6. **The counter-intuitive twist (0:42–0:53):** But [ultimate threat makes the current strategy impossible], so you [relocate or flip the core logic: hide in plain sight, or weaponize a hazard]. And that [key feature] is not there to [assumed purpose]. It's there to [actual, reversed purpose].
7. **Atmospheric dressing and dry payoff (0:53–1:00):** Add [small functional prop], [dramatic lighting or environmental detail], and [one absurd or relatable minor nuisance]. Congratulations, you have built [final title subject].

## Production and pacing checklist
- **Cut cadence:** a new camera angle, cutaway or asset spawn on every "but" and "so you", roughly every 3–5 s.
- **No dead air:** one steady, deadpan read with no dramatic pauses until right before "Congratulations".
- **Visual-only nouns:** replace abstract words ("security", "comfort", "camo") with physical objects you can model ("iron bars", "lantern", "pine branches").
- **Length:** about 210–220 words gives about 60 s at the 1.25x voice.
- **House additions:** Gus is "you". Cousin Steve appears, often as the minor nuisance. The "key feature" in the twist must be something planted in an earlier loop.
