      GUARD Hold back to block HIGH & MID. Hold down-back to block LOWs (crouching ducks HIGHs).

      PARRY Tap parry just before a hit lands (10-frame window). Negates damage, staggers the attacker, reflects projectiles and opens a COUNTER window (+25% dmg). Whiffing leaves you open.

      COMBOS Basic attack chains 3 hits. Land a basic, then cancel into any ready skill. Launchers pop foes airborne for juggles. Damage scales down over long combos.

      STATUS BLEED / BURN tick damage • STUN (dizzy, can't act) • BLIND (-30% dmg) • CONFUSE (left/right swap) • SLOW • RATTLED (-15% dmg).

      STANCES Cycle with STANCE: BALANCED • AGGRESSIVE (+15% dmg, faster, take +10%, tighter parry) • DEFENSIVE (take -15%, no chip, wider parry, slower, -12% dmg).

      SUPER METER Fills as you deal and take damage, parry and taunt. Half a bar: press EX then a skill for a powered-up EX skill (+35% dmg). Full bar while being comboed: EX = COMBO BREAKER.

      THROWS Forward + PARRY up close. Beats blocking and parrying; attacks beat throws; throwing at the same time = THROW TECH.

      MORE RAGE under 25% HP (+10% dmg) • FIRST ATTACK each round +10% • hold a direction when knocked down to TECH ROLL • blocking too much fills your guard gauge until it BREAKS • TAUNT for meter (you're open while you do it).

      NEW CLASH: two skills collide — mash ATTACK to break through • ITEMS: frying pan / chair (3 powered hits), energy drink (speed + meter), banana peel (slip trap) • EVOLVED fighters' ultimates become super-ultimates (+50%).

      STAGES Rooftop: smash someone into the wall to break through to the street • Arcade: grab JACKPOT power-ups • Classroom: knock foes through desks.

      AIR Double jump once per jump. DOWN + ATTACK in the air dives into a slam with a ground shockwave that knocks down.

      K.O. The round-winning blow triggers a slow-motion finisher. Best of 3 rounds, 99s timer.
