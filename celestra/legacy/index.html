Tekken-Inspired System Mechanics

Standing Guard (Hold Back)Blocks HIGH and MID attacks. Vulnerable to LOW sweeps.
Crouching Guard (Down-Back)Blocks LOW attacks and ducks under HIGH jabs. Hit by MID kicks & overhead slams.
Back-Dash Cancel (BDC)Double-tap Back to back-dash, tap Down to cancel recovery, and repeat rapidly to create space.
Launchers & JugglesHit with Launcher (O/L) to pop foes airborne, then chain Light/Heavy/Signature hits mid-air!
Close Throw (Fwd + Launcher)Breaks turtles! Unblockable grab when close (within 85px).
Jab Strings (1-2-3)Press Light → Light → Heavy rapidly for a fast target combo chain.
